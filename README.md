 Telecom Customer Churn Analysis SummaryKey Demographics & Retention: While the total volume of senior citizen customers is lower, a proportionately larger percentage of senior citizens churn compared to non-seniors.
 Looking at customer loyalty, new users are highly unstable; people tend to stay once they survive past the first 1–2 months of service.
 Services & Contract Risk: Customers with a Month-to-month contract show a massive likelihood to churn compared to those locked into stable 1 or 2-year agreements.
 Furthermore, using Fiber optic internet or lacking crucial companion features like OnlineSecurity, OnlineBackup, and TechSupport strongly correlates with high churn counts.
 Payment Methods: Financial billing habits play a major role in customer loss, as individuals paying via Electronic check are significantly more likely to churn than those using automatic transfers or mailed checks.
